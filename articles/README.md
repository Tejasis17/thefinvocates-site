Drop your articles (.md or .html) and PDFs in this folder, then push.
The filename becomes the title on the site — e.g.
"RBI-Master-Direction-KYC-2026.pdf" shows up as "RBI Master Direction KYC 2026".

This file itself won't be picked up by the site (it isn't a .md/.pdf/.html
content file in the sense the loader expects an actual article) — replace or
remove it once you've pushed your first real piece.
